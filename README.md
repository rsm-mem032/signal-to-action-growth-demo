# Signal-to-Action Growth Demo

A reusable frontend case-study demo for showing how AI-assisted growth teams can turn fragmented external signals into qualified opportunities and human-reviewed actions.

## What it shows

- Signal monitoring across multiple public channels
- Relevance filtering before deeper review
- AI-assisted qualification for users, creators, and partners
- Action recommendation for growth or GTM follow-up
- Human-in-the-loop review instead of unchecked automation

## Good fits

This template can be adapted for:

- Creator platforms
- SaaS products
- Early-stage AI startups
- Community-led products
- Partnership and GTM workflows
- Growth ops case studies

## How to customize

The site is driven by two editable layers inside `app.js`:

1. `siteConfig`
   Controls the page copy, headings, metrics, workflow steps, examples, and tags.

2. `opportunities`
   Controls the sample dashboard cards, scores, labels, and recommended actions.

To adapt this template for a different company or case:

- change the `brand` and `hero` sections in `siteConfig`
- update `summaryCards`, `whyNow`, `workflow`, and `examples`
- replace the `opportunities` array with company-specific sample signals
